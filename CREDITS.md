# Credits and licenses

## Original assets

The portrait and WebP research/event photographs in `public/images/` are optimised copies of photographs supplied by Haytham Hussein. The selected originals are archived in `source/photos/` and can be processed again with `scripts/optimise_photos.py`. Confirm permission for photographs showing other people before publishing.

The remaining original SVG illustrations in `public/images/`, the favicon in `public/icons/`, and the generated Open Graph image were created specifically for this project. They contain no third-party photographs or stock imagery.
